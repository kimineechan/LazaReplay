# LazaReplay v1.2.0

LazaReplay can now record your microphone along with what the computer plays.

## New

- **Microphone**, under **02 Settings**. Choose **Default microphone**, which is whichever one Windows is set to use, or one microphone by name. Your voice is added to the computer's sound, in the same sound track and in step with it.
- No microphone is recorded unless you choose one. The list starts at **Off**, and your settings from an earlier version leave it off.
- With **Record what the computer plays** switched off and a microphone chosen, clips have your voice alone.
- If the microphone is unplugged or Windows blocks it, recording carries on without it and the Sound line says why.

Everything else is as in 1.1.0.

## Worth knowing

- A microphone that is muted, on the headset or in Windows, records as silence. If your voice is missing, check the mute switch, or choose the microphone by name.
- Your voice and the computer's sound are mixed when they are recorded and cannot be balanced in the clip afterwards.
- With speakers in place of headphones, the microphone hears the speakers too.

## Download

Download `LazaReplay-v1.2.0-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, and open `LazaReplay.exe`. It targets 64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11. No development tools or installer are required. If an earlier version is running, exit it first from the menu of its icon by the clock.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the hashes of the ZIP and of both programs. The ZIP also includes the programs' checksums.

## Compatibility

Tested on Windows 10 build 19045 with two microphones. The window is a little taller than in 1.1.0. AMD graphics chips, NVIDIA desktop cards and Windows 11 have not been tested by the author. Run `lazareplay-check.exe` to see how LazaReplay fares on your computer, and include its report in any bug report.

See `VALIDATION.md` for the checks that were run and where they stop.
